Fitness · Boxing

Fight IQ

Your personal boxing coach, inside your front camera.

Prop your phone up, step back and start throwing punches. Fight IQ places targets for jabs, hooks and uppercuts around you on screen. Hit them as if a coach were holding the pads, and train solo anytime, anywhere.

  • Real-time punch targets using the front camera
  • Guided rounds and combos for every level
  • Workouts that adapt to your progress
  • No equipment, no gym, no partner needed

Creativity · Family

Wondraw

Every line is a little magic.

Draw anything, even a simple doodle, and watch it come to life. After every stroke, Wondraw instantly re-imagines the drawing as a beautiful picture in the style you choose. Kids see their ideas grow line by line.

  • Live AI magic that updates with every line you draw
  • 14 art styles, from storybook to watercolour
  • A personal gallery saved on your device
  • Simple, friendly tools designed for families

Games · Party

Move IT

You are the controller.

Move IT turns your phone into a motion-gaming console. Set the phone near your TV, step back and play with your whole body: slice fruit with your hands, dodge obstacles by leaning and ducking, and squeeze through walls by copying their shapes.

  • Full-body tracking with just the phone's camera
  • Slice Party, Winter Dodge and Pose Wall
  • Play on your phone or on the big screen
  • Party Cup mode for friends and family
