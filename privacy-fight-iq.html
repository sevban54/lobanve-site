Fight IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Provided by Lobanve LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Lobanve LLC ("Lobanve", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Fight IQ mobile application (the "App") on Android and iOS. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The front camera is used only to detect your body and punches in real time.
  • Camera images are processed on your device. They are not recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Your training progress is stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your data.

Camera

Fight IQ asks for permission to use your device's front camera. The camera feed is analysed on your device, in real time, to estimate the position of your body and hands so the App can show punch targets and count your hits. Video frames are held in memory only for the moment they are analysed and are then discarded. We do not record photos or video, and camera images are never sent to us or to any third party. You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your device settings; the training features will not work without it.

Information stored on your device

The App stores your workout history, progress, preferences and settings locally on your device so it can adapt your training. This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Purchases and subscriptions

If the App offers paid features, purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only the information needed to confirm your purchase, such as a transaction identifier and subscription status.

Diagnostics

Google Play and the App Store may provide us with aggregated, anonymous crash and performance reports if you have enabled sharing on your device. These help us fix problems and do not identify you personally.

Health and fitness disclaimer

Fight IQ is for general fitness and entertainment and is not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, make sure you have enough free space around you, and stop if you feel pain or discomfort.

Children

Fight IQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email us at sevban54fidan@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for any data that leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
