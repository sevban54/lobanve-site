Move IT Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Provided by Lobanve LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Lobanve LLC ("Lobanve", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Move IT mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The camera is used only to track your body movements while you play.
  • All camera processing happens on your device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • No account, no ads, and we do not sell your data.

Camera and body tracking

Move IT asks for permission to use your device's camera. The App uses on-device body-pose detection to estimate the positions of your joints so you can control the games with your body. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately. We do not record photos or video, and camera images are never sent to us or to any third party. You can turn off camera access at any time in your device settings.

Playing on a TV

If you choose to play on a TV, the App sends the game picture (the game graphics, not your camera image) to your TV or streaming device over your local network using screen mirroring or Google Cast. This connection is between your own devices and does not pass through our servers.

Information stored on your device

Scores, best results, settings and calibration preferences are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Purchases

If the App offers paid features, purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Safety

Move IT involves physical movement. Make sure you have enough clear space, keep children supervised and stop if you feel pain or discomfort.

Children

Move IT can be enjoyed by families. Because the App does not collect personal information or send camera data off the device, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because Move IT keeps your data on your device, uninstalling the App removes it. For any question or request, email us at sevban54fidan@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
