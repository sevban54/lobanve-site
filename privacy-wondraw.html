Wondraw Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Provided by Lobanve LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Lobanve LLC ("Lobanve", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Wondraw mobile application (the "App") on Android and iOS. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • To create the "magic" picture, your drawing and the chosen art style are sent securely to our server and our AI image-generation provider.
  • Drawings are used only to create the picture for you. They are never used in advertising.
  • Your gallery, credits and settings are stored on your device.
  • We do not ask for a child's name, photo, email or location, and we do not sell data.

Information we process

Drawings and art styles. When you draw, the App sends the current drawing and the selected art style over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to our AI image-generation service provider to create the re-imagined picture. The picture is then returned to the App. Drawings are processed only for this purpose. They are not used to identify anyone and are not used for advertising. We and our provider keep them only as long as needed to generate and deliver the picture, plus a short period for security and abuse prevention, unless a longer period is required by law.

Information stored on your device. Your gallery (drawings and their pictures), credits and preferences (such as who is drawing, theme and language) are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App, or using the delete option in the Profile screen, removes this information.

Photos. If you choose "Save to Photos", the App asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It does not read your existing photos.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive what is needed to confirm and manage your purchase. Purchases are made with the device owner's (parent's) store account and are protected by the store's parental controls.

Advertising measurement

We do not show third-party ads in the App. Only if the adult device owner allows it through the system permission prompt, we may use an advertising identifier to measure whether our own marketing campaigns bring new families to Wondraw. You can decline or change this at any time in your device settings. Drawings and pictures are never used for advertising.

Service providers

We use carefully selected providers to run the App: cloud hosting for our server and an AI image-generation provider that creates the pictures. They may process data only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us.

Children's privacy

Wondraw is designed for families and may be used by children under a parent's supervision. We aim to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as a child's name, contact details, photos, voice or precise location. Drawings are processed without being linked to an identity. Parents can contact us at any time to ask questions or request deletion of any information related to their child.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Drawings sent for processing are not kept longer than described above. To request deletion of any data we may hold, email us at sevban54fidan@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email us at sevban54fidan@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data that leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
